Artisan Story +

Arjun Kumar is a third-generation blue pottery artisan from Jaipur, Rajasthan. He learned the craft from his father at the age of 12 and has been perfecting his techniques for over two decades. His workshop employs 5 local craftspeople, helping preserve this traditional art form.

Arjun specializes in contemporary adaptations of classic designs, blending traditional patterns with modern aesthetics while staying true to authentic materials and techniques.
