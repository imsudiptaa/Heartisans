Our Story

Heartisans began in 2022 when our founder, Vihaan Desai, traveled across rural India meeting skilled artisans who struggled to find markets for their exceptional handcrafted products. Despite creating items of remarkable quality and cultural significance, these craftspeople often earned less than fair wages due to exploitative middlemen and limited market access.

Inspired by their resilience and creativity, Vihaan envisioned a platform that would directly connect these artisans to global markets while ensuring authenticity, fair compensation, and sustainability. With a background in technology and a passion for cultural preservation, he assembled a team of experts in AI, e-commerce, and sustainable development.

Today, Heartisans has grown from a small startup to a global marketplace representing over 2,500 artisans from 45 countries. We've pioneered innovative features like AI-powered authentication, a sustainable resale marketplace, and verified auction platforms that ensure the continued value and appreciation of handcrafted items.

Our journey is just beginning as we continue to expand our community, innovate our technology, and deepen our impact across the global artisan economy.
