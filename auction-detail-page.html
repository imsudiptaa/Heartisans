Description

This exquisite wooden sculpture represents a fine example of 19th century Bengali craftsmanship. Carved from a single piece of teak wood, it depicts a traditional scene from rural Bengal with intricate details and expressive figures.

The piece was discovered during the renovation of an old haveli in North Kolkata and has been carefully restored by master artisan Rajesh Kumar. The patina and natural aging of the wood have been preserved to maintain its historical authenticity.

The sculpture shows sustainable craftsmanship techniques of the period, with the wood sourced from naturally fallen trees. This piece is not only a beautiful decorative item but also a valuable cultural artifact representing Bengal's rich artistic heritage.
